## Selected corrections and interpretive cautions

- The prose unit occasionally calls Bond’s story The Kite Runner; the prescribed story is The Kite Maker. The Kite Runner is a different work by Khaled Hosseini.
- Ratna is Jairaj’s wife in Dance Like a Man. Nittilai is a tribal woman in The Fire and the Rain.
- The Guide leaves the ending open to interpretation. Shame evokes a fictionalised Pakistan; it should not be treated simply as a history of India.
- Some rasa glosses in the source are misleading: hasya concerns laughter and shanta tranquillity. Ramanujan’s contexts are analytical distinctions, not timeless national stereotypes.
