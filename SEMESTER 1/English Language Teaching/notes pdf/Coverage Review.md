## Selected corrections and interpretive cautions

- Fixed visual/auditory learning-style matching is not treated as an established teaching rule; use flexible support and learner needs.
- LAD and universal grammar are theoretical constructs, not physical organs. Krashen’s hypotheses are influential models rather than uncontested laws.
- Reliability and validity are distinct. Frequent testing becomes formative when its evidence changes teaching or learning.
- Native-speaker-only materials are not required for authentic learning. Historical claims about examination formats should be checked against current test providers.
