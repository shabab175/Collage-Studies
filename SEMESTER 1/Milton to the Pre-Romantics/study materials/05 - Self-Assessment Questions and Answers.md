Self Assessment Questions and Answers
 1. Who authored The School for Scandal?
 ANSWER: Richard Brinsley Sheridan
 2.Which diary gives an intimate account of Restoration society?
 ANSWER: Samuel Pepys’s Diary
 3. Which act in 1707 unified England and Scotland?
 ANSWER: The Act of Union
 4. Which poem by Pope is considered a mock-epic?




 ANSWER: The Rape of the Lock
 5. Who became James I of England in 1603, uniting the crowns of Scotland
    and England?
 ANSWER: James VI of Scotland
 6. Which essay periodical shaped 18th-century public opinion?
 ANSWER: The Spectator
 7. Which epic by Milton aimed to ‘justify the ways of God to men’?
 ANSWER: Paradise Lost
 8. Which satirical allegory did Dryden write based on a biblical revolt?
 ANSWER: Absalom and Achitophel
 9. Who was the first Hanoverian monarch of Britain?
 ANSWER: George I
 10. In which year was Charles I executed?
 ANSWER: 1649
 11.Which two political parties emerged during the Exclusion Crisis?
 ANSWER: Whigs and Tories
 12.Who were known as Cavalier poets?
 ANSWER: Robert Herrick, Thomas Carew, Richard Lovelace, John Suckling
 13. Which act formalised Parliamentary supremacy after 1688?
