## Selected corrections and interpretive cautions

- The contents page labels The School for Scandal as poetry; it is a play, first performed in 1777, later than early Restoration comedy.
- Paradise Lost Book I ends with the gathering in Pandemonium; the extended debate occurs in Book II. Satan’s persuasive voice is distinct from the narrator’s.
- Some Burns questions and references confuse A Red, Red Rose with Ae Fond Kiss. Use the actual prescribed poem rather than inventing a reason for parting.
- Claims about a uniquely first English novel or a fixed fifty-year lifespan of the periodical essay are contested simplifications.
