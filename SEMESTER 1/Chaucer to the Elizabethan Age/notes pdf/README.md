# Expanded exam notes — PDF editions

Coverage-reviewed editions: all 15 units, with targeted supplementary details and the unit assignment prompts included. Editable text is retained in `expanded sources/`.

One PDF per textbook unit. These editions retain the accessible chapter explanations and add longer exam-style discussions, answer structures, and common mistakes. They are more detailed than the short Markdown notes and substantially shorter than the original textbook.

The expanded discussions are examples of how to develop ideas, not quotations from the textbook or guaranteed exam answers. Use the full study materials for exact quotations and further textual evidence.

[Read the coverage review](Coverage%20Review.md) for what was added, what was checked, and what still needs the full textbook.
