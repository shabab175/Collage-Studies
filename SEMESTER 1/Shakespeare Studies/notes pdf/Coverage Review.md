# Coverage review — Shakespeare Studies

## What is complete

The subject package follows all 15 units in the supplied SGOU MA English course M21EG02DC. It contains the original PDF, full extracted unit files, simple teaching notes, expanded exam PDFs, editable PDF sources, and 15 illustrated revision posters. The expanded PDFs total 60 pages; each unit is 4 pages, approximately 980–1,200 words including the revision checklist.

## What was checked

- The original has 332 PDF pages. The extraction assigns pages 1–332 once each across the units and supporting sections. This establishes page allocation, not perfect recognition of every image or character.
- All 15 units have matching simple notes, expanded sources, PDFs, and poster images.
- The prescribed Hamlet, Twelfth Night, Sonnets 18, 55, 130 and 144, The Tempest, Antony and Cleopatra, Rosencrantz and Guildenstern Are Dead, Shylock Is My Name, and Ran are explicitly explained. The four critical-response units receive separate treatment rather than being folded into plot summaries.
- All 93 numbered unit assignment prompts are included as PDF revision checklists. Counts were compared against each source unit before text reflow. These checklists are not 93 fully written answers and do not predict an examination.
- The expanded PDFs include teaching explanations, a developed analytical discussion, an answer plan, and a common-mistake note. Text was extracted again from every PDF to confirm those sections survived rendering.
- All posters were opened for visual review. Two captions were corrected before the final files were saved: Ophelia’s description and the closing guidance on the Sonnet 130/144 poster.
- Sample PDF pages, including a long chapter title and an end section, were rendered and visually inspected. All new local index links and file counts were checked.
- The original PDF copy was checked by SHA-256 against the upload.

## Source cautions and interpretive qualifications

1. **Jacobson’s title:** the published title is Shylock Is My Name. The course sometimes reverses it to “My Name is Shylock.” The notes and filenames use the published title; assignment prompts retain the course’s wording where appropriate.
2. **Jones’s book:** Hamlet and Oedipus is a 1949 book, not an 1899 publication. A psychoanalytic reading is presented as a critical hypothesis, not a proven diagnosis or Shakespeare’s own declaration.
3. **Character descriptions in the Jacobson unit:** the compressed course account contains inconsistencies and should not be treated as an independently verified character dictionary. The notes avoid relying on its unsupported widower description or treating every character as a mechanically exact Shakespearean equivalent.
4. **Sonnets and biography:** the identities behind Mr. W. H., the Fair Youth, and the Dark Lady are not presented as settled. Thorpe’s involvement does not by itself prove either piracy or full authorial control. Sonnet 99, 126, and 145 are exceptions to aspects of the usual formal pattern.
5. **Genre:** romance and problem-play categories are critical tools with a history; the plays can mix modes. Roman setting and tragic form are not mutually exclusive labels.
6. **Characters’ claims:** the notes distinguish a character’s or lyric speaker’s view from confirmed events, the narrator’s position, and the author’s beliefs. This matters especially for prejudice, jealousy, gender, and imperial stereotypes.
7. **Ran:** this is not a one-to-one character substitution exercise. Hidetora’s violent past, altered family relationships, and cinema’s visual and aural methods must be analysed as changes. An interpretation in an assignment question is not automatically an uncontested fact.

These are selected factual cautions and critical qualifications. This review is not an independent verification of every textbook statement, reference-list entry, or historical claim.

The Malayalam university song is not reliably represented by the PDF’s text encoding. Its extracted characters are retained without pretending they are a corrected transcription or translation. Consult the original page image for that section.

## How to prepare a longer exam answer

Use the simple notes to understand the chapter, the expanded PDF to practise connected paragraphs, and the original textbook and assigned primary texts for exact quotations and close analysis. For Ran, also watch the relevant sequences: a visual description in notes does not replace the film.

The condensed PDFs do not reproduce every biography, footnote, stanza, scene, bibliographical item, or possible examination argument. They are structured revision aids, not a replacement for reading the prescribed works.
