## General instructions to learners

- Clearly articulate your main argument in a concise manner at the beginning of your answer.
- This sets the tone for your analysis and helps the reader understand the central point you are trying to convey.
- Support your analysis with specific examples and quotes from the literature. Use direct textual evidence to illustrate and reinforce your points.
- Move beyond mere summary and engage in critical analysis. Interpret the significance of the literary elements, such as themes, characters, and symbols
- The assignment being prepared should contain a minimum of 10 pages

## Questions — choose any one

1. Examine Shakespeare’s use of dramatic techniques such as soliloquy, imagery, dramatic irony and disguise in creating psychological depth.

2. Analyse Twelfth Night as a comedy of love, disguise, mistaken identity and social transformation.

3. Analyse the themes of love and immortality in Shakespeare’s prescribed sonnets.

4. Examine The Tempest as a play concerned with power, betrayal, forgiveness and reconciliation.

5. Analyse Rosencrantz and Guildenstern Are Dead as an absurdist play, highlighting its use of uncertainty, circularity, humour, and existential themes.
