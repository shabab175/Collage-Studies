## Questions — choose any one

1. Describe the social, political and cultural environment of Elizabethan England and its influence on Shakespeare’s works.

2. Describe the major classifications of Shakespearean plays with suitable examples.

3. Describe Hamlet as a Shakespearean tragedy, with particular reference to its tragic elements, Hamlet’s character, and the theme of revenge.

4. Describe Howard Jacobson’s Shylock Is My Name as a modern reworking of Shakespeare’s The Merchant of Venice.

5. Describe how Akira Kurosawa’s Ran adapts the story and themes of Shakespeare’s King Lear to the cultural context of feudal Japan.
